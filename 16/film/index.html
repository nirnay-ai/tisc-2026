Solar Headroom Desk: the demo film

Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026, challenge 16: DER and Rooftop-Solar Management. About 3 minutes.

The film walks through the prototype's own screens, with an English voice and English captions. The prototype runs on public data. Its transformers are synthetic, built on the utility's published counts, and the screen says so. The WhatsApp input shown in the film is planned for a pilot; it is not built yet.
