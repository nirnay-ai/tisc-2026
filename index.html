Four working prototypes for Telangana's power network

Our entries for the Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026, Energy. Each prototype is one file that runs in the browser, on public data. Where a utility's own records are not public, it uses a synthetic network and says so on the screen.

08challenge

Feeder Planner

Network Mapping, Sectionalizing and Load Switching

Which distribution transformers and 11 kV lines overload at the next peaks, which switching brings customers back after a fault, and which works to fund first within a budget.

10challenge

Substation Solar Planner

Solar Substation Potential Assessment

Every extra-high-voltage substation assessed on the same things: room on the grid, land that could be suitable, sunshine. The viable ones are ranked, with a card for each.

16challenge

Solar Headroom Desk

DER and Rooftop-Solar Management

How much more rooftop solar each distribution transformer and feeder can take, which transformers pass their cap within a year, and the next day's rooftop output by hour.

17challenge

Storm Desk

Storm / Weather Outage Prediction and Crew Dispatch

Where faults are likely, where crews should wait before the storm, and whom to send first.
